Questions about the gold rate near you

What is the gold rate near me today?

On 31 October 2026 the median rate across the 36 jewellers we track is ₹13,903 per gram for 24K, ₹12,744 for 22K and ₹10,427 for 18K, before 3% GST and making charges. National chains charge the same rate everywhere; regional chains set their own, so the rate at a jeweller near you can differ by a few hundred rupees a gram.

How do I find the cheapest gold rate near me?

Use your location above to open the page for your nearest city. It lists the national chains and the regional chains serving your state, cheapest 22K first, with today's rate from each.

Is the gold rate the same at every jeweller near me?

No. Each jeweller sets its own daily rate, and making charges vary even more, from a few percent on plain chains and coins to 20% or more on detailed designs. Ask for the rate, the making charge and the hallmark (HUID) before you buy.

Do you use my location?

Only in your browser, to pick the nearest city on this page. It is not sent to us or stored.

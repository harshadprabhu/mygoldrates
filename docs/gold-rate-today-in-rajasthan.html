What is the IBJA gold rate and why does it differ from jeweller rates?

The IBJA (India Bullion and Jewellers Association) publishes India's twice-daily bullion reference rate. Jeweller board rates typically sit slightly above it, reflecting sourcing and hallmarking premiums.

Are these gold rates inclusive of GST?

Rates shown are per gram, pre-GST, so brands can be compared on the same basis. Use the GST switch on the comparison table, or the calculator, to see prices including 3% GST. Making charges vary by design and are always extra.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K and 18K gold?

24K (99.9% pure) is investment-grade gold used for coins and bars. 22K (91.6%) is the standard for traditional Indian jewellery. 18K (75%) is harder and common in diamond and everyday jewellery. Purity scales the price: the 22K rate is 91.6% of the pure-gold rate.

How often are these rates updated?

Our team updates the rates manually every day. Each brand's figure is checked against the market before it is published, and subscribers receive the day's comparison by email.

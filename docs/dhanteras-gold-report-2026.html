Dhanteras 2026: what the same gold jewellery costs at each jeweller

Gold rates as of 12 November 2026 · making charges measured 2026-11-01

Dhanteras fell on Friday, 6 November 2026. It is the busiest day of the year for gold buying, and most buyers compare one number before they go: the gold rate. This report compares the number that decides the bill. Every price below is for 10 grams of 22K gold - gold value at the jeweller’s own published rate, plus its making charge, plus 3% GST.

Key findings
  • A 22K bangle weighing 10 g costs ₹1,51,134 at Khazana Jewellery and ₹2,26,680 at Kisna once making charges and 3% GST are added - a difference of ₹75,547 (50%).
  • The gold rate itself barely differs: Reliance Jewels and Waman Hari Pethe, the lowest and highest 22K rates among these jewellers, are ₹4,763 apart per 10 g, while the bill for the same 22K bangle weighing 10 g differs by ₹75,547. Making charges, not the rate, decide the price.
  • The lowest gold rate does not buy the cheapest jewellery: Reliance Jewels has the lowest 22K rate of these jewellers, but Khazana Jewellery works out cheapest for a 22K bangle weighing 10 g.
  • Making charges run from 14% to 68% of the gold value across the jewellers and items in this report.
  • The 22K market median has moved from ₹13,157/g on 20 July to ₹12,934/g today (-1.7%), -5.6% over the last 30 days.

How these numbers are made

Gold rate: each jeweller’s own published 22K rate per gram, pre-GST, on 2026-11-12. Making charge: the median making charge, as a percentage of gold value, across real products in that category on the jeweller’s own website, read from each product’s published price breakup. The Products column is how many were measured; figures marked * rest on a smaller sample. Total = gold value + making charge + 3% GST on both.

Left out because too few products could be measured to give a reliable median: Candere (bangle), GRT Jewellers (mangalsutra), GRT Jewellers (ring), Manubhai Jewellers (bangle), Manubhai Jewellers (bracelet), Manubhai Jewellers (chain), Manubhai Jewellers (earrings), Manubhai Jewellers (mangalsutra), Manubhai Jewellers (necklace), Manubhai Jewellers (pendant), Manubhai Jewellers (ring), NAC Jewellers (bangle), NAC Jewellers (ring).

Making charges vary by design, and in-store offers and exchange terms are not included, so treat these as like-for-like indications rather than quotes. This is not investment advice.

Dhanteras 2026: what the same gold jewellery costs at each jeweller

Gold rates as of 28 October 2026 · making charges measured 2026-10-16

Dhanteras falls on Friday, 6 November 2026 - 9 days from today. It is the busiest day of the year for gold buying, and most buyers compare one number before they go: the gold rate. This report compares the number that decides the bill. Every price below is for 10 grams of 22K gold - gold value at the jeweller’s own published rate, plus its making charge, plus 3% GST.

Key findings
  • A 22K pair of earrings weighing 10 g costs ₹1,56,650 at GRT Jewellers and ₹2,23,300 at Kisna once making charges and 3% GST are added - a difference of ₹66,650 (43%).
  • The gold rate itself barely differs: PN Gadgil and CaratLane, the lowest and highest 22K rates among these jewellers, are ₹2,365 apart per 10 g, while the bill for the same 22K pair of earrings weighing 10 g differs by ₹66,650. Making charges, not the rate, decide the price.
  • The lowest gold rate does not buy the cheapest jewellery: PN Gadgil has the lowest 22K rate of these jewellers, but GRT Jewellers works out cheapest for a 22K pair of earrings weighing 10 g.
  • Making charges run from 16% to 64% of the gold value across the jewellers and items in this report.
  • The 22K market median has moved from ₹13,159/g on 20 July to ₹13,138/g today (-0.2%), -4.2% over the last 30 days.

How these numbers are made

Gold rate: each jeweller’s own published 22K rate per gram, pre-GST, on 2026-10-28. Making charge: the median making charge, as a percentage of gold value, across real products in that category on the jeweller’s own website, read from each product’s published price breakup. The Products column is how many were measured; figures marked * rest on a smaller sample. Total = gold value + making charge + 3% GST on both.

Left out because too few products could be measured to give a reliable median: Candere (bangle), GRT Jewellers (mangalsutra), Waman Hari Pethe (mangalsutra).

Making charges vary by design, and in-store offers and exchange terms are not included, so treat these as like-for-like indications rather than quotes. This is not investment advice.

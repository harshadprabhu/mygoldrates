Dhanteras 2026: what the same gold jewellery costs at each jeweller

Gold rates as of 05 November 2026 · making charges measured 2026-11-01

Dhanteras falls on Friday, 6 November 2026 - 1 days from today. It is the busiest day of the year for gold buying, and most buyers compare one number before they go: the gold rate. This report compares the number that decides the bill. Every price below is for 10 grams of 22K gold - gold value at the jeweller’s own published rate, plus its making charge, plus 3% GST.

Key findings
  • A 22K bangle weighing 10 g costs ₹1,57,856 at Khazana Jewellery and ₹2,33,103 at Kisna once making charges and 3% GST are added - a difference of ₹75,247 (48%).
  • The gold rate itself barely differs: Reliance Jewels and Waman Hari Pethe, the lowest and highest 22K rates among these jewellers, are ₹1,902 apart per 10 g, while the bill for the same 22K bangle weighing 10 g differs by ₹75,247. Making charges, not the rate, decide the price.
  • The lowest gold rate does not buy the cheapest jewellery: Reliance Jewels has the lowest 22K rate of these jewellers, but Khazana Jewellery works out cheapest for a 22K bangle weighing 10 g.
  • Making charges run from 14% to 68% of the gold value across the jewellers and items in this report.
  • The 22K market median has moved from ₹13,157/g on 20 July to ₹13,385/g today (+1.7%), -2.8% over the last 30 days.

How these numbers are made

Gold rate: each jeweller’s own published 22K rate per gram, pre-GST, on 2026-11-05. Making charge: the median making charge, as a percentage of gold value, across real products in that category on the jeweller’s own website, read from each product’s published price breakup. The Products column is how many were measured; figures marked * rest on a smaller sample. Total = gold value + making charge + 3% GST on both.

Left out because too few products could be measured to give a reliable median: Candere (bangle), GRT Jewellers (mangalsutra), GRT Jewellers (ring), NAC Jewellers (bangle), NAC Jewellers (ring).

Making charges vary by design, and in-store offers and exchange terms are not included, so treat these as like-for-like indications rather than quotes. This is not investment advice.

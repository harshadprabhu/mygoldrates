Gold Rate FAQs

What is the gold rate today in India?

On 23 July 2026, the market median 24K gold rate across major Indian jewellers is ₹14,648 per gram (pre-GST). The 22K rate is ₹13,431 per gram. Rates are compared across 14 leading jewellery brands and updated daily.

Which jeweller has the lowest gold rate today?

Today, PN Gadgil lists the lowest effective 24K gold rate at ₹14,436 per gram. Jewellers' rates typically differ by 1-3% because each brand embeds slightly different premiums in its pricing.

What is the gold rate today in Hyderabad?

The gold rate today in Hyderabad closely tracks the national jeweller rate. On 23 July 2026, the median 24K (999) rate is ₹14,648 per gram and 22K (916) is ₹13,431 per gram, pre-GST. Most national chains quote a uniform price across their Hyderabad stores, so the 14-brand comparison on this page applies in Hyderabad.

What is the gold rate today in Chennai?

On 23 July 2026, the gold rate today in Chennai is in line with the national median of ₹14,648 per gram for 24K and ₹13,431 per gram for 22K (pre-GST). Chennai buyers can use the comparison board and calculator above to check each jeweller's rate before purchase.

What is the gold rate today in Pune?

The gold rate today in Pune mirrors the pan-India jeweller rate. As of 23 July 2026, 24K gold is around ₹14,648 per gram and 22K is ₹13,431 per gram (pre-GST) across the brands compared here, which price uniformly across their Pune outlets.

What is the gold rate today in Mumbai?

On 23 July 2026, the gold rate today in Mumbai matches the national median of ₹14,648 per gram (24K) and ₹13,431 per gram (22K), pre-GST. Because leading chains quote one rate across all their Mumbai showrooms, this 14-brand comparison applies in Mumbai too.

What is the IBJA gold rate and why does it differ from jeweller rates?

The IBJA (India Bullion and Jewellers Association) 24K reference rate today is ₹14,556 per gram for 999 gold, before GST. Jewellery brands price on average +0.7% above the bullion reference today; the gap reflects each brand's sourcing and hallmarking premium.

Are these gold rates inclusive of GST?

Rates shown are per gram, pre-GST, so brands can be compared on the same basis. Use the GST switch on the comparison table, or the calculator, to see prices including 3% GST. Making charges vary by design and are always extra.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K and 18K gold?

24K (99.9% pure) is investment-grade gold used for coins and bars. 22K (91.6%) is the standard for traditional Indian jewellery. 18K (75%) is harder and common in diamond and everyday jewellery. Purity scales the price: the 22K rate is 91.6% of the pure-gold rate.

How often are these rates updated?

Our team updates the rates manually every day. Each brand's figure is checked against the market before it is published, and subscribers receive the day's comparison by email.

About MyGoldRates

Last updated 03 August 2026

MyGoldRates is an independent gold rate comparison platform for India. Every day we track the 24K, 22K and 18K gold rate published by the country's leading jewellery brands and display them side by side on a single screen, normalised to the same basis — per gram, pre-GST — so any buyer can compare them honestly and make a more informed decision before purchase.

The problem we set out to solve

Gold is one of the most significant financial decisions most Indian families make. The average Indian household holds more gold than most investment portfolios: weddings, festivals, family heirlooms, and savings all translate into physical gold. Yet buying gold in India has always been an information asymmetry problem.

Every jeweller quotes a slightly different rate. Some embed a portion of making charges into the headline gold rate; others show a lower base rate but add high making charges at billing. GST of 3% is added on top at the counter. The IBJA (India Bullion and Jewellers Association) publishes a daily bullion benchmark, but most buyers have never heard of it. The result: most people walk into a jeweller without knowing whether the quote they receive is competitive or not.

We built MyGoldRates to fix that. The goal is simple: a buyer who spends two minutes on this site should walk into any jewellery store with a clear sense of what today's rate should be, which brands are pricing below the median, and roughly what their purchase should cost before making charges and GST.

What we track and how

We monitor the published gold rates of more than 19 national jewellery brands daily. These include all major national chains — Tanishq, Malabar Gold, Kalyan Jewellers, Joyalukkas, PC Jeweller, Senco Gold, Bhima Jewels, Orra, PNG Jewellers, CaratLane, and others. Rates are checked and updated multiple times each day during market hours.

To make comparison meaningful, we normalise every rate to a common basis: 24K (999 fineness), per gram of gold, before 3% GST. This removes the distortions that make cross-jeweller comparison confusing in the wild. Alongside the jeweller comparison, we display the IBJA bullion reference rate and the AKGSMA (All Kerala Gold & Silver Merchants Association) rate for South India, so buyers can see how jeweller prices compare to the wholesale benchmark.

For details on our data collection process, see our methodology page.

Our tools

Beyond the daily rate comparison, we offer a suite of calculators designed for real purchase decisions:

  • Price calculator: Enter weight, purity and making charge to estimate the exact billed cost of any jewellery piece.
  • Making charges comparison: Category-by-category data on what leading jewellers actually charge for making — rings, bangles, chains, earrings, and more — based on real product price breakups.
  • Gold loan calculator: Estimate loan amount, interest and monthly outflow against gold collateral.
  • Gold SIP calculator: Model systematic gold investment returns over time.

Who we are

MyGoldRates was founded by a small team of technology professionals and finance enthusiasts based in India who were frustrated by the lack of transparent, comparable gold pricing information for everyday buyers. None of us are affiliated with any jewellery brand or bullion dealer. We have no commercial relationship with any jeweller whose rates appear on this site — we do not receive commission, referral fees, or any other payment from them. The rates we show are taken from each jeweller's own publicly published information.

Our editorial independence matters to us. If a jewellery brand's rate appears higher than the median on a given day, we show it higher. If a brand is pricing below the field, their customers benefit from that transparency too. We do not alter, round, or editorially adjust any rate.

Editorial standards

We are committed to accuracy and transparency in everything we publish. Our specific commitments:

  • No rate manipulation: Rates displayed are exactly as published by each brand, converted to our standard basis (24K/g/pre-GST). We do not round, adjust, or interpolate rates to favour any brand.
  • Clear data freshness: Every page shows when rates were last updated. On days when a brand has not published a rate by our collection time, we mark that brand's entry clearly.
  • Pre-GST basis: All rates are shown before GST. We clearly state this on every page. Buyers should add 3% for the actual billed rate.
  • No investment advice: Gold rates are informational only. We do not advise on whether to buy, sell, or hold gold. Gold prices fluctuate and past rates do not guarantee future prices.
  • No affiliation disclosure: We have no commercial relationship with any jeweller featured on this platform.

Accuracy and limitations

While we aim for the highest possible accuracy, there are inherent limitations to any gold rate comparison service. Jewellers may update their rates intraday; the rate you see on their website at 9 AM may differ from what they quote in-store at 3 PM. Jewellers in different cities sometimes quote slightly different rates for the same brand due to local market conditions or inventory. Making charges, gemstone costs, and GST are always additional to the gold rate and vary significantly by design.

We therefore always recommend confirming the exact billed rate directly with the jeweller before finalising any purchase. Our comparison is a starting point for informed decision-making, not a substitute for the final bill.

Coverage and scope

We currently track national jewellery chains that operate across India. We are actively working to expand our coverage to include more regional brands in South India, East India, and smaller cities. If there is a brand you would like us to add, or if you notice a rate discrepancy, please contact us — we review all such reports promptly.

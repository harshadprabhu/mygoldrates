Gold Rate FAQs

What is the gold rate today in India?

On 21 July 2026, the market median 24K gold rate across major Indian jewellers is ₹14,423 per gram (pre-GST). The 22K rate is ₹13,225 per gram. Rates are compared across 14 leading jewellery brands and refreshed daily.

Which jeweller has the lowest gold rate today?

Today, PN Gadgil lists the lowest effective 24K gold rate at ₹14,291 per gram. Jewellers' rates typically differ by 1-3% because each brand embeds slightly different premiums in its pricing.

What is the IBJA gold rate and why does it differ from jeweller rates?

The IBJA (India Bullion and Jewellers Association) reference rate today is ₹14,340 per gram for 999 gold and ₹13,136 per gram for 916 gold, before GST. Jewellery brands price on average +0.7% relative to the bullion reference today; the gap reflects each brand's sourcing and hallmarking premium.

Are these gold rates inclusive of GST?

Rates shown are per gram, pre-GST, so brands can be compared on the same basis. Add 3% GST for the billed price of the gold value. Making charges vary by design and are always extra.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K and 18K gold?

24K (99.9% pure) is investment-grade gold used for coins and bars. 22K (91.6%) is the standard for traditional Indian jewellery. 18K (75%) is harder and common in diamond and everyday jewellery. Purity scales the price: the 22K rate is 91.6% of the pure-gold rate.

How often are these rates updated?

Rates are refreshed automatically several times every day, and each brand's figure is quality-checked against the market before it is published.

Questions

Which is cheaper today, Malabar Gold or Senco Gold?

Malabar Gold is cheaper today: 22K at ₹13,160 per gram against ₹13,179 at Senco Gold - ₹19 less per gram, ₹189 on 10 g. For a 10 g 22K ring, after each jeweller's making charge, its making offer and 3% GST, Malabar Gold works out about ₹18,010 cheaper (₹1,58,456 at Malabar Gold, ₹1,76,466 at Senco Gold).

What is the making charge at Malabar Gold and Senco Gold?

Malabar Gold: bangle 16.9%, earrings 16.9%, mangalsutra 16.9%, ring 16.9%; Senco Gold: bangle 27%, coin 5%, earrings 30%, mangalsutra 27%, ring 30%. Medians of plain 22K pieces read from each jeweller's own price breakup; your design will differ.

Is gold at Malabar Gold and Senco Gold the same purity?

Yes. Both sell BIS-hallmarked jewellery: 22K is 91.6% gold (stamped 916) and every piece carries a 6-digit HUID you can check in the BIS Care app. The rate difference is the jeweller's own pricing.

How is the final bill worked out?

Gold value (rate x weight) + making charge (a % of the gold value, less any offer) + 3% GST on both, plus a small hallmarking fee. Confirm the exact figure with the jeweller.

Gold rate in Jaipur: common questions

What is the gold rate today in Jaipur?

On 28 October 2026 the median gold rate across the 33 jewellers we track is ₹14,332 per gram for 24K (999), ₹13,138 for 22K (916) and ₹10,749 for 18K (750), before 3% GST and making charges.

Which jeweller has the lowest gold rate for Jaipur today?

Among the national chains and the regional chains serving Rajasthan that published a rate today, PN Gadgil is lowest for 22K at ₹13,036 per gram, and CaratLane highest at ₹13,272, a gap of ₹236 per gram.

How much do 10 grams of 22K gold cost in Jaipur with GST?

At today's 22K median of ₹13,138 per gram, 10 grams cost ₹131,377 before tax and ₹135,318 with 3% GST. Jewellery also carries a making charge, usually quoted as a percentage of the gold value or per gram, which is added before GST.

Is the gold rate the same at every jeweller in Jaipur?

No. Each jeweller sets its own daily rate, and making charges differ even more than rates, so compare both before buying.

Where do people buy gold in Jaipur?

The main gold markets are Johari Bazaar, MI Road, Vaishali Nagar.

Does the gold rate in Jaipur change every day?

Yes. Jewellers revise their rates each morning, and some again during the day, following the international gold price and the rupee. We refresh this page several times a day.

FAQs

What is today's gold rate?

The hero rate on the Rates tab shows the live per-10g price for the selected jeweller and karat, computed from the current international gold spot price. Switch karat (24K/22K/18K) or tap any brand row to see its rate.

Which jeweller has the lowest rate today?

Brands are sorted lowest-to-highest on the Rates tab; the top row is tagged "Lowest". The savings chip shows how much per gram you'd save versus the highest-priced brand shown.

What's the difference between IBJA and jeweller rates?

IBJA (India Bullion and Jewellers Association) publishes a national bullion reference rate. Individual jewellers price slightly above it to cover margin, purity assurance and overheads — that gap is shown as the delta (Δ) next to each brand.

Is GST included in these rates?

No, all rates are shown pre-GST by default. Tap "+3% GST" to add it to every price on screen, including the hero rate and calculators.

How often are rates updated?

The live badge at the top refreshes automatically about once a minute from a live gold-spot feed, and you can tap it any time to force an immediate refresh.

What's the difference between 24K, 22K and 18K gold?

Karat measures purity: 24K is ~99.9% pure gold, 22K is ~91.6% (916), and 18K is 75%. Lower karat jewellery costs less per gram but contains less actual gold — use the karat pills to compare.

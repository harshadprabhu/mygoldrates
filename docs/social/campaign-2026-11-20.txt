Gold tumbles 📉 MCX gold is ~Rs 1,22,500 (20 Nov, per 10 g) - Broke below the Rs 1.25 lakh support. World gold is near $3,520/oz, 14-month low.

WHY IT'S FALLING:
1. Fed rate-HIKE bets - Markets price a ~95% chance of a US rate hike in December; one governor floated 50 bps. Gold pays no interest, so higher rates weigh on it.
2. A 19-month-high dollar & 5% yields - The dollar is near a 19-month high and the 10-year US yield is above 5% - both pull dollar-priced gold down.
3. Worst year since 2015 - Gold is ~33% below its early-2026 record and having its worst year since 2015; profit-taking and margin calls add to the selling.

LEVELS: Support that broke today Rs 1.25 lakh / $3,650; Next level being watched Rs 1.22 lakh / $3,500-3,475; Off the early-2026 record ~33%.

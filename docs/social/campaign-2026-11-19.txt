Why is gold falling? 📉 24K is ₹13,069/g today, down ₹256 in a day and 20% below the 24 Aug peak. World gold is near a 14-month low.

1. US rate-hike bets - Markets expect the Fed to raise rates again. Gold pays no interest, so it loses appeal.
2. A stronger dollar & yields - The dollar and US bond yields are up. Gold, priced in dollars, gets pushed down.
3. Profit-taking after the record - World gold is ~32% below its early-2026 peak near $5,600; big rallies get sold.
